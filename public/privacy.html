Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Rx Vault ("we") helps you store prescriptions and medical reports for yourself and your family. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and name.
  • Health records you choose to upload: images and PDFs of prescriptions and reports, with their type, date and title.
  • Family profiles you create and the sharing permissions you approve.

How we use it

Only to provide the app: storing your records, showing them to you and to people you have approved. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use it for tracking or analytics.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase in the Mumbai (India) region, encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Our service provider can technically access stored data; access is restricted to operating the service.

Sharing

Your records are visible only to you, to people you manage profiles for, and to people you approve through an invite. You can remove anyone's access at any time in the Family screen.

Your rights

You can view, correct and delete your data in the app. Settings → Delete account permanently deletes your account, your records and all uploaded files. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

Children

Profiles for children are created and managed by a parent or guardian, who consents on their behalf.
